The email uses a single flat background color, no gradients. Dark mode colors are derived automatically from these three colors.

    How it works

    Choose a color palette, add your brand name, headline and up to six products, and the newsletter updates as you type. Check it on desktop, mobile and in dark mode, then copy the HTML.

    The code is built with email-safe tables, inline styles and an Outlook fallback, so it renders consistently in Gmail, Outlook and Apple Mail. Paste it into any email platform that accepts custom HTML, such as Mailchimp or Brevo.

    Everything runs in your browser. Your texts and links are not sent anywhere.
